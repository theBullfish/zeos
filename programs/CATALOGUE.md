## Methodology

For each program:
1. **Write it** in Tier 3 Z+
2. **Try alternate approaches** — is there a better wiring?
3. **Log what worked** — which constructs felt natural
4. **Log what's missing** — what the language needed but didn't have
5. **Log what's ambiguous** — where the spec wasn't clear enough
6. **Estimate conventional LOC** — for comparison
